Good to know

Before you get in touch.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within one working day. If you have not heard back in two, please WhatsApp me — it means the message did not arrive.

Where do you teach?

Online, anywhere in South Africa, on Microsoft Teams. I am based in Gauteng and adjudicate nationally for the Allegretto Inter-Provincial Eisteddfod, but the coaching itself is not tied to a location.

Do you teach in Afrikaans?

Yes. Coaching and speech writing are both available in English and Afrikaans, including Afrikaans eisteddfod sections and ATKV Redenaars.

What does the free assessment involve?

Twenty minutes on Teams. The student performs or reads something — anything — and we talk. You leave knowing where they stand and what would help. There is no sales pitch at the end.

What do we need for the first call?

A laptop, tablet or propped-up phone with a camera, somewhere with light on the learner's face, and about two metres of floor. Full setup notes here.
