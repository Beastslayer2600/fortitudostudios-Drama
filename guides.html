Guides

Performance notes for students who want the gold-standard finish.

Use these guides before class, auditions, and eisteddfod performances.

Guide 01

How to prepare a monologue

  1. Read the full piece twice before making acting choices.
  2. Mark where the thought changes, not only where the sentence ends.
  3. Choose who you are speaking to and what you want from them.
  4. Rehearse standing up so your body learns the piece too.
  5. Polish the first ten seconds until the opening feels calm and controlled.
Guide 02

Eisteddfod performance checklist

  1. Know the title, author, and time limit.
  2. Practise your entrance, stillness, introduction, and exit.
  3. Keep costume choices neat, readable, and performance-friendly.
  4. Project to the back wall without shouting.
  5. Finish strongly, hold the final moment, then leave with confidence.
Guide 03

Audition confidence

  1. Arrive knowing your piece, your character, and your first line.
  2. Use your breathing to slow down before you begin.
  3. Make bold choices, but keep them truthful.
  4. If you make a mistake, stay in character and continue.
  5. Thank the panel and leave the room professionally.
