Questions

Before you book.

What age do you take from?

Group classes run from Grade R upward, banded by age so learners work with their peers. Private coaching has no lower limit — I have prepared learners as young as six for their first eisteddfod item, and adults preparing for auditions or professional speaking.

Does my child need experience?

No. The Confidence & Expression group assumes no background at all. If a student is nervous, that is usually the reason to start rather than a reason to wait.

Can drama really be taught online?

For competition preparation, yes. An adjudicator watches a still, framed performance from a fixed distance — which is close to what a camera sees. Recording every session is a real advantage: the learner watches themselves the way the adjudicator did. What online will not give you is a full stage rehearsal with movement across a large space, and I will say so if that is what a piece needs. There is more detail here.

Do you guarantee results at an eisteddfod?

No, and be cautious of anyone who does. What I guarantee is that the piece will be well chosen, correctly timed, properly interpreted and performed to a standard that gives the student the best honest chance on the day.

You adjudicate for Allegretto — how does that work if my child competes?

Every learner I coach is declared to the organisers in advance. At provincial and national level, sections are judged by a panel of several adjudicators, so where a learner of mine is entered my mark is one of several rather than the deciding one. Where I am sole adjudicator, I do not judge my own clients. The full policy is published here.

Can we start mid-term?

Yes. Group fees are pro-rated to your start date. Private coaching can start as soon as there is a slot.

Do you teach in Afrikaans?

Yes — English and Afrikaans, including Afrikaans eisteddfod sections and ATKV Redenaars.

What about in-person coaching?

Everything is online for now. That is a deliberate choice, not a limitation: it keeps fees lower, removes travel for both of us, and means I can work with learners anywhere in the country rather than only in Gauteng.
