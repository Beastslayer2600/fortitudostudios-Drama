Disclosure

Adjudication, coaching and disclosure.

I adjudicate for the Allegretto Inter-Provincial Eisteddfod, I coach learners who enter it, and I write speeches for competition. Here is exactly how those overlap and what I declare.

Applies to all work undertaken by Fortitudo Studios Drama · Published August 2026 · Reviewed annually

The reason to choose this studio is that I adjudicate. That same fact raises an obvious question, and parents deserve a straight answer rather than a reassuring silence. This page is that answer. It is public so that you can hold me to it — and so that the parent of a competitor I do not work with can read it too.

1. Everything is declared in advance

I keep a current register of every learner I coach and every speech I write, in any format. That register goes to the organising committee before each event — not in response to a query afterwards. Organisers therefore always know, ahead of time, exactly where my commercial work and my adjudication might intersect, and can allocate sections accordingly.

Disclosure is not something I do when asked. It is the first thing I do.

2. How panel adjudication works

At provincial and national level, sections are adjudicated by a panel of several adjudicators, not by one person. Where a learner I coach is entered in a section I sit on, I adjudicate as one member of that panel and my mark is one of several. I am not the deciding voice, and no single adjudicator on a panel can determine a placing alone.

That structure is the substantive protection, and it is why panel adjudication exists. Combined with advance declaration, it means the organisers know the position, the other panellists know the position, and the result rests on more than my opinion.

Where I am sole adjudicator of a section, I do not judge a learner I coach or have written for.

3. On written speeches

I write speeches for public speaking, debate and group public speaking. This is a normal and permitted part of the South African competitive speaking landscape — the published rules for the major circuits encourage learners to write their own material in the experimental and original-composition categories, but do not require it generally. A number of established services operate on exactly this basis.

Two limits I place on myself:

  • Where a section requires or is designated for the learner's own original composition — experimental categories, own-composition eisteddfod items — I coach only. I will not write material for a section whose whole purpose is the learner's own authorship, and I will tell you if the section you are entering is one of those.
  • I tell you to disclose it if you are asked. If a school, teacher or organiser asks who wrote a speech, answer honestly. Nothing about this arrangement needs concealing, and a family caught being evasive about something permitted looks far worse than one that simply says so.

4. What no fee buys

No payment to this studio affects any result anywhere. Marks reflect the performance on the day, assessed against the published criteria, by a panel.

  • I will not discuss other entrants, panel composition or likely results. If you ask, I will decline.
  • I will not accept work that is framed, however lightly, as buying an advantage in a result.
  • A learner I coach who performs poorly will be marked as having performed poorly. That is the only version of this that is worth anything to either of us.

5. What I ask of you

  • Tell me at the outset which eisteddfods and competitions the learner intends to enter, so that I can declare accurately and early.
  • Tell me if entries change during the year.
  • If you are ever unsure whether a section requires the learner's own composition, ask me before we start. I would rather redirect you to coaching than have you enter the wrong section with the wrong material.

6. Raising a concern

If you believe anything on this page has been departed from, contact me directly at gertjiefourie@icloud.com or on 072 243 6950. You are equally entitled to raise it with the relevant organising committee without speaking to me first, and I would not regard doing so as a discourtesy.

Why this is published

An overlap that is declared, documented and structurally diluted is manageable. One that is quietly hoped-about is not. Publishing this costs me nothing so long as I follow it, and it gives every parent — client or not — something concrete to measure me against.
